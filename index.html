About this page

What is real and what is simplified
  • Every trade is a real transaction on your fork, sent to the real Uniswap contracts. The sandbox signs it for one of its test accounts (Alice, Bob, Carol, …).
  • When you connect, the page gives each trader 5,000,000 USDC and 5,000 AAVE and lets the Uniswap routers spend them, by writing the token contracts' storage, the same trick as SandboxLab's Fund wallet. It then takes a snapshot: the start point.
  • For Uniswap v3 the curve uses the pool's virtual reserves, which follow x · y = k only inside the current price range. The stats also show the tokens the pool really holds.
  • To put several transactions in one block, the page switches auto-mining off, sends them, mines one block, and switches it back on. The block orders them by priority fee, as a block builder would.
  • Carol's front-running bot only watches the Uniswap v2 pools. Gas costs are shown, but the bot ignores them when it picks its trade size.
