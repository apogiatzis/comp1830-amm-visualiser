AMM Visualiser

Two real transactions from the night of the Binance hack, 29 October 2026, recorded from Ethereum mainnet. Nothing here runs on your sandbox: this page shows what the network recorded, the same data a block explorer or SandboxLab's transaction page shows. Pick a transaction, then read its swaps, transfers, call trace and balance changes.

Swaps

Every Swap event, in the order they happened. Amounts are what went into and came out of each pool.

Token and ETH transfers

Every token Transfer event and every call that moved ETH, in order.

Call trace

Each line is one contract calling another; indented lines happen inside the call above them. Events (amber) appear inside the call that emitted them.

ETH balance changes

Accounts whose ETH balance changed in this transaction (before → after), gas fee included.
